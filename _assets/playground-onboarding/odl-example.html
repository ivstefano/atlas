Why some cells are empty. Four of the fourteen are equipment or process patents rather than material patents — bridge-girder welding, waste-heat recovery and two oil-and-gas filings — so they have no alloy composition to extract. Blanks are left visible rather than hidden: this is what real output looks like.

Every value is checkable. Open any of these patents and the numbers are there. For JP6374864B2 the filing states C 0.12–0.18%, Mn 1.9–2.2%, a tensile strength of at least 780 MPa and elongation of at least 14% — in Japanese.
