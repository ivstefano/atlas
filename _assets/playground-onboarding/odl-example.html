Patent column stays pinned while the rest scroll. 14/14 rows carry a measured property; 10/14 carry a composition.

Why some cells say "not stated". Four of the fourteen are equipment or process filings rather than material filings: bridge-girder welding, waste-heat recovery, and two oil-and-gas patents. They have no alloy composition to extract, so the cell is left empty rather than filled with a guess. The columns that apply to every document are populated for every document.

The numbers are checkable. For JP6374864B2 the filing states C 0.12-0.18%, Mn 1.9-2.2% and a tensile strength of at least 780 MPa at 14% elongation, in Japanese. Open the PDF and every value in that row is on the page.
