Two honest observations

What this means in practice

i.

The outer ring is impressive but invisible from outside

A hiring manager reading "Ivo built his own engagement memory system and an OCR-first extraction pipeline" hears side project, not this is why he scopes twice as fast. For external positioning the inner circle is the headline; the outer circle is the proof.

ii.

The middle ring is where IRIS gets the most leverage and Ivo is under-titled

Vankata holds vision, Jordan holds the deal narrative, Viktor holds the strategy. Ivo holds the operational glue across all three. That isn't in any standard SA job description, and it's the line worth raising in any comp or title conversation.

At a Salesforce-scale company Ivo would be a user of these systems. Here he's their architect.
