Question 1 · Overall feedback on the period

Successes, challenges, how the last six months felt

The form's open question: overall feedback on performance and experience over the past six months, including successes, challenges, personal improvements, and how I felt.

Successes

Delivery that moved deals. Built the Aumovio extractor V2 pipeline to 99.6% F1 on reviewed docs, well ahead of the alternative on the same corpus. Ran the DB InfraGO scoping demo to 96.6% groundedness in a single cycle. OCR'd and extracted the full 100-doc Aumovio corpus.

Platform leverage that compounds. Stood up the engagement-folder convention, the deployment patterns, and the automation layer (Pyloth clone, state system, morning briefs). One SA now covers the work of roughly 1.2 roles, and a second SA could ride the same rails from day one.

Breadth. Carried 15+ engagements across scoping, POC, demo, and handover without dropping the thread on any of them.

Challenges & how it felt

The role outgrew the title. I came in as a Software Architect and spent the half doing Solutions Architect work. Rewarding, and also a genuine stretch: the commercial muscle is not the same as the engineering one.

Commercial-call presence is the honest gap. Vankata was direct about it and he is right. The May 19 Heineken RAG-sync misframe worried Marti and Bobi, a concrete example of framing wobbling under pressure. That is the H2 focus.

Multi-hat load. Demo, scoping, PM, tech-lead in the same week is energising but leaves no single dimension at best-in-class. Generalist by design, with the trade-off that comes with it.

Net read: the technical half has been the easier half, and it is where I delivered. The half that turns an SA from "useful in the room" into "moves the deal" is commercial coverage: speaking with the right confidence, framing in the listener's language, holding presence when a call turns adversarial. I know where the edge is, I accept the feedback, and the infrastructure to scale my output is already in place.
