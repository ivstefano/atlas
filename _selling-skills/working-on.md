## The two skills Jordan named (2026-07-10, Postbank no-show sideways)

Jordan's frame: I started technical, moved commercial. That's the hard direction to travel, so it's a structural advantage — someone who starts commercial needs years to get the tech. Press the advantage by adding exactly two skills. Capturing both = "good to great, plus-player SA territory."

Both are **structure skills**, which is the same leverage the whole framework already bets on: structure beats fluency. I don't need more range. I need the structure reflex to fire live, not after Jordan pulls it out of me.

### Skill 1 — Walk backwards from the commercial destination
> "Keep a laser focus on the actual end commercial destination or the opportunity. And how do we walk backwards from that to get the shortest number of steps required."

Amazon's "invent and simplify." Default to fewer steps; justify every step against the money.

- **What it looks like when I fail it:** I lead with the mechanism instead of the destination. HubSpot permissions ask (this call): I said "developer permissions, read only" and Jordan had to ask "for what?" three times before I produced the actual goal (pull HubSpot locally, diff my state vs. what needs updating, like I do for Asana).
- **The rep:** before any ask or proposal, write the destination FIRST, then the shortest path. One sentence: *"I want read access to deals + projects so I can diff HubSpot against my local state and self-correct — same as Asana. Cleanest way to grant that?"*
- **Add to drills.md before-call habit:** alongside "3 claims as what/when/who," add "for any ask, destination-first then shortest path."

### Skill 2 — Executive stakeholder management + meeting structure (one joint skill)
> "How do I speak at the executive/leadership level... and structure these meetings so we get the exact right thing we need."

- **What it looks like when I fail it:** meetings that don't extract the one thing they exist to extract. Jordan's example: three Postbank calls, "60% has been stuff they already said in each call." (Not my fault this time — but it's the failure mode I now own preventing.)
- **The rep:** before every client call, write the ONE thing this meeting must produce. If I can't name it, the meeting isn't ready. After the call: did I get that one thing, or did we rehash? The scoping-intake questionnaire ([scoping-intake-questionnaire.md](scoping-intake-questionnaire.md)) is the structural answer for scoping calls — it moves factual discovery out of the room so the call spends only on what needs a human.
- **Stakeholder sub-skill (delivery, not just content):** "Just don't tell him he's going into the dumb valley" (this call) — I correctly saw Vanka is over-complicating and Jordan should push back, then softened it into a joke. The instinct to manage how the message lands is right; execution defaulted to deflection. Rep: state the framing crisply, don't joke past it.
