# Drills — repetition to build the habit

Skill comes from reps, not reading. Short, frequent, on real material.

## Daily (5 min)
Pick one [pattern](talk-like-jordan.md). Take 3 things I'd actually say this week (a Slack update, a line in a call, a status note) and rewrite each using the pattern. Out loud once.

## Before every client call (2 min)
Write my 3 key claims as **structured claims** (Pattern 1): what / by when / who decides. If I can't fill all three for a claim, I don't yet understand it well enough to say it — go find the missing anchor.

## After every client call (3 min) → log in call-log.md
- One line I said that landed.
- One line that came out vague/Steven-ish — rewrite it the way I should have said it.
- Did I state the gap-as-plan? (y/n) If no, what did I overclaim?

## Weekly review (10 min)
Read the week's call-log. Which pattern is sticking? Which still defaults to vague? Drill the weak one next week.

## Imitation source material
Jordan's real messages are the textbook. When he posts a status (e.g. the Aumovio wrap-up in #active-engagements), copy the structure: status → what's left framed as plan → who decides → dated next steps. Re-tell one of MY engagements in that exact shape.

## On automated LinkedIn cold-outreach (selling AI SaaS) — PARKED, on purpose

I asked whether this skill could power automated LinkedIn messages to sell AI SaaS. Short answer: **not yet, and probably not the way it sounds. Here's the honest reasoning so future-me doesn't reopen it lazily.**

**Why it fights the skill I'm building:**
- My entire edge is **proof-by-delivery** and **honesty-as-credibility** (Vankata) — high-trust, specific, one-to-one. Automated cold blasts are the *opposite*: low-trust, generic, one-to-many. Bolting my proof-based style onto a spam channel wastes the very thing that makes me good.
- The skill I want is **talking** — live, in the room, reading the person. Cold-DM automation doesn't train that; it trains copywriting a template once. Different muscle.

**Why it's risky on its own terms:**
- LinkedIn automation violates their ToS; accounts get restricted/banned. Burning my real LinkedIn (my proof/credibility asset) for volume is a bad trade.
- Mass outbound about "AI SaaS" we don't fully have as one platform = exactly the overclaiming gap that started this whole thread. Automating the vapor-version of the pitch is the worst possible direction.
- It's outward-facing + at scale → falls under the never-auto-send rule. Anything like this is draft → Ivo approves → send, never automated fire-and-forget.

**What WOULD be useful instead (if/when I want outbound):**
- A **drafting aid**, not an autosender: I feed it a *specific* person + their context, it helps me write ONE genuine, proof-led message I personally review and send. Manual, high-quality, low-volume. That trains the skill and protects the asset.
- Warm follow-ups to people I actually met (the LTW/VivaTech lead lists Jordan/Steven keep) — personalised, referencing the real conversation. That's not cold, and it's where deals actually come from.

**Decision:** build the talking skill first (this folder). Revisit a *manual drafting aid* later if I have real outbound need. Do NOT build an autoposter. If future-me wants to, re-read this paragraph first.
