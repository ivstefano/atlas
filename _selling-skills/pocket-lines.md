# Pocket lines — my 20

The fix for "I don't have things I've learned to say": Jordan isn't improvising. He runs a **memorized repertoire** that *sounds* free because he's said each line 1000 times. I don't have a talent gap — I have an empty pocket. Fill it.

**How to use:** memorize VERBATIM. Say each out loud 10x until it comes without thinking. Goal = retrieval, not composition. I already understand them; I need them automatic.

Grouped by situation. ⭐ = my weakest area (stating a gap) — drill these hardest.

---

## A. Opening / proof-by-delivery (lead with receipts)
1. "We built exactly this for an automotive client — validated at 99.6% against their own expert's numbers."
2. "I'm not going to pitch you theory — let me show you something we already shipped."
3. "The shortest version: we've done this before, on harder data than yours."

## B. ⭐ Stating a gap (turn weakness into a plan — MY HARDEST, drill most)
4. ⭐ "That's a known item — it gets solved in phase 1 of implementation, not in the POC."
5. ⭐ "Today it doesn't do that yet. The path to it is clear and it's on the roadmap — here's when."
6. ⭐ "I'd rather be straight with you: that part isn't built yet. Here's what is, and here's the plan for the rest."
7. ⭐ "There's about 10-15% of cases that still need work. That's expected at this stage, and it closes in 1-2 iterations."
8. ⭐ "What you saw is real. What's *not* real yet is the self-serve layer — that's the build we're proposing."

## C. Real vs roadmap (Vankata's honesty = credibility)
9. "Today it does X. Y is on the roadmap. I'll make sure delivery matches exactly what we sell."
10. "Let me be precise about what's live versus what we'd build for you."
11. "Right now we run and manage that for you. Handing it over to your team is a later phase, by design."

## D. Buying time / not overclaiming
12. "Let me confirm the exact number and come back to you — I'd rather be precise than fast."
13. "Good question. I don't want to guess — I'll get you the real answer by [day]."
14. "I can give you a rough sense now, and the exact figure by [day]. Which do you want?"

## E. Structuring next steps (Jordan's what/when/who)
15. "So next step: [thing], by [date], and [name] decides. Did I get that right?"
16. "Let's land 3 concrete deliverables, then extend from there once those work."
17. "Who on your side makes the final call on this? I want to make sure we're building for the right person."

## F. Closing / handling pushback
18. "What would you need to see to be confident this works for you?"
19. "If we prove [X] in the POC, is that enough to move to implementation?"
20. "Fair concern. Here's how we de-risk it: [one concrete step]."

---

## Drill priority order (do in this sequence)
1. Group B (⭐ gaps) — my weakest, highest value.
2. Group C (real vs roadmap) — pairs with B, protects me at acceptance.
3. Group E (next steps) — easy structure win.
4. Then A, D, F.
