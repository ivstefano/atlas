# Talk like Jordan (in my own voice)

Jordan isn't persuasive because of fancy words. He's persuasive because **every claim has structure**. I don't need "MECE" or "Minto pyramid" or native-English polish. I need 4 patterns. Plain English (even ESL) is fine — structure beats fluency.

Drill these until they're automatic. One real example each, from my own work.

---

## Pattern 1 — Structured claim: WHAT / BY WHEN / WHO DECIDES
Never say a vague claim. Attach the three anchors.

- ❌ "We're making good progress on the extraction."
- ✅ "Extraction is validated at 99.6% on the 17 reviewed docs. Remaining 10-15% lands in implementation phase 1. Phil signs off, then Norbert takes it to management."

> Jordan's own: "30k commit from Garrett, FID by 23 July, Phil takes it to Norbert Monday."

**My template:** `[thing] is [concrete status]. [next thing] by [date]. [named person] decides.`

---

## Pattern 2 — Gap-as-plan (turn a weakness into a roadmap item)
I already know the real gaps (that's my strength). State them as *plan*, not *deficit*.

- ❌ "It doesn't handle the x2 caliber cases yet."
- ✅ "The x2 caliber cases are a known item — solved in 1-2 iterations during implementation, not POC scope."

Same facts. One sounds broken, one sounds planned. **This is my single highest-value borrow.** It sells AND protects me (on-record that I never hid the gap).

**My template:** `[gap] is a known item — [how it gets closed] in [phase], not [current scope].`

---

## Pattern 3 — Proof-by-delivery (my unique weapon, use it constantly)
I have receipts. Most sellers don't. Lead with what I actually shipped.

- ✅ "We built exactly this for an automotive client — OCR-first extraction pipeline, validated against their expert's ground truth at 99.6%. Same approach applies here."

Clients buy *shipped*. When the room is full of vision-talk, I'm the trust anchor because I'm the one who's done it.

**My template:** `We built exactly this for [client/case] — [concrete result]. Same applies to you because [link].`

---

## Pattern 4 — Credibility line (Vankata's move = honesty that sells)
Say what's real vs roadmap, out loud. It makes the rest of my words trusted.

- ✅ "Today, the context layer is something we generate and manage for you. Self-serve editing is roadmap — phase 2. I'll make sure delivery matches exactly what we sell."

The last sentence is the two-birds line: sells trust + protects me at acceptance.

**My template:** `Today it does [real X]. [Y] is roadmap, [phase]. I'll make sure delivery matches what we sold.`

---

## What to SKIP (not my voice, not my strength)
- Steven's abstraction: "blue ocean," "stickiness," "version of the truth," "up the value chain." Don't. It floats above the work and the gap I'm nervous about lives exactly there.
- Faking native-English smoothness. Structure > fluency. A clear ESL sentence with what/when/who beats a smooth empty one.
