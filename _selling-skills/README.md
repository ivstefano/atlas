# Selling skills — Ivo

My space to deliberately build selling/communication skill. I already deliver (3 projects sold-then-shipped). The gap is **selling and how I talk**, not capability.

## Core thesis (don't lose this)
I sell by **proof**, not by **vision**. I have receipts most salespeople don't: things I actually shipped. My edge is being the one person in the room who has built the thing. So I don't copy Steven (vision/abstraction) — I copy:
- **Jordan's structure**: every claim = *what / by when / who decides*. Plain words, no jargon needed.
- **Vankata's honesty-as-credibility**: state exactly what's real vs roadmap; that makes clients trust the enthusiasm when I do turn it on.

My honesty is a **selling asset**, not a weakness. The discomfort I feel about overclaiming is the instinct that makes Vankata's style land. Lean into it.

## The two-birds principle
Stating "today it does X, Y is phase 1, here's the timeline" is **both** a selling move (Jordan's gap-as-plan) **and** self-protection (written record that I never sold vapor). The selling skill and protecting myself within a strategy I can't change are the *same* sentence. Always say the gap as a plan, on the record.
