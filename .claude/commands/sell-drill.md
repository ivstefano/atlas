# Sell drill

Ivo is building selling skill by **repetition**. His edge is proof-by-delivery + honesty (not vision). The point of this drill is to move pocket lines from "understood" to "automatic retrieval" — like Jordan, who sounds free because he's drilled his repertoire.

Source files (read them, don't reinvent):
- `atlas/_selling-skills/pocket-lines.md` — the 20 lines, grouped A-F. Group B (⭐ gaps) is his weakest.
- `atlas/_selling-skills/talk-like-jordan.md` — the 4 patterns.
- `atlas/_selling-skills/call-log.md` — append-only reps log (create if missing).

Argument: `$ARGUMENTS`

## Behavior

Keep it SHORT and active. This is a 3-5 min rep, not a lecture. One drill per invocation.

### Pick which line(s) to drill
- **empty**: auto-pick. **Bias hard toward Group B (gaps)** — ~50% of the time pick a ⭐ line, since that's his weakest. Otherwise rotate C → E → A → D → F. Don't repeat the line drilled in the last call-log entry.
- **`gap`** → a Group B line. **`proof`** → A. **`roadmap`** → C. **`next`** → E. **`close`** → F.
- **`review`**: skip drilling. Read the last ~7 days of call-log.md, tell him which pattern is sticking and which still defaults to vague, and name the ONE line to drill next. Then stop.

### The drill (default path)
1. Show him the ONE line, verbatim.
2. Give him a **real situation** to use it in — pull from his ACTUAL current work, not invented. Look at the current engagement (cwd or recent _state / STATUS), or a live deal (Garrett, Heineken, Aumovio, Fresenius). E.g. "Garrett asks on the call: 'so it extracts everything automatically today?' — respond using line 6."
3. Ask him to type or say his version out loud.
4. When he answers: react like a tough but fair sales coach. Did it have what/when/who? Did he state the gap as a *plan* not an apology? Did he avoid Steven-style vapor? One concrete fix, one thing he did well. Keep it to 3-4 lines.
5. Append a one-liner to `call-log.md`: date, line drilled, his attempt, the one fix. (Create call-log.md with a header if it doesn't exist.)

### Coaching rules
- He's ESL. **Never** push fluency or fancy words. Structure > smoothness. A blunt clear sentence wins.
- His instinct toward honesty is the asset — reinforce it, never tell him to soften the truth to close.
- If his attempt overclaims (says a thing is built when it's roadmap), flag it directly — that's the exact habit this whole effort exists to fix.
- End by telling him the next `/sell-drill` will hit [area], so he knows the loop continues.
